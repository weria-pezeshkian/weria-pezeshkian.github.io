## Fabian receives a Veni grant

Fabian has been awarded an NWO Veni grant for his project **“How mutations alter the very beginning of Alzheimer’s disease.”** He will use computer simulations to explore how mutations in the amyloid precursor protein influence the earliest molecular events linked to Alzheimer’s disease.
